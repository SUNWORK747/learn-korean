-(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 Not Only... But Also

The Formal, Emphatic Upgrade for Addition — Closing Out the Cause/Condition Cluster

Learning Goals ① Understand -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 as an emphatic addition connective meaning "not only X but also Y," and recognize that 뿐 is a bound noun originally meaning "only" — so 뿐(만) 아니라 literally negates "only"

③ Distinguish -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 from the basic connective -고, recognizing -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 as the formal, emphatic, written-register ("문어체") upgrade for addition and enumeration

1Concept Introduction — When "And" Isn't Emphatic Enough

Since Elementary Korean, you've been able to add one fact to another with the simple connective -고 ("A and B"). But formal writing — essays, news articles, official reports, academic papers — often needs something stronger: a way to say "not only is A true, but B is also true, and that's worth emphasizing." That's exactly the job of -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라, one of the most common advanced connectives for building persuasive, layered arguments in 문어체 (written/literary register). Instead of just listing two facts side by side, it frames the second fact as an addition on top of an already-notable first fact — "not only X, but Y as well."

The key to understanding this pattern is the word 뿐 itself: a 의존명사 (bound noun) that, on its own, means "only" or "merely" (e.g. 예쁠 뿐이다, "it is only that [something] is pretty"). -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 takes that same 뿐, adds the emphatic particle 만 ("even"), and negates it with 아니라 ("is not") — literally "it is not even merely the case that..." This chapter is also the last of the "인과·조건 고급 표현" (Advanced Cause/Condition Expressions) cluster you've been working through since GRAM-ADV-012; the next section moves on to 추측·전문(傳聞) 고급 표현 (Advanced Conjecture/Hearsay Expressions), starting with GRAM-ADV-016.

Preview
이 제품은 가격이 저렴할 뿐만 아니라 품질도 좋다. ("This product is not only affordable, but its quality is also good.")
학생뿐만 아니라 교사도 이 프로그램에 참여할 수 있다. ("Not only students but also teachers can participate in this program.")
The first sentence attaches -(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 to the adjective stem 저렴하다; the second attaches 뿐만 아니라 directly to the noun 학생. In both cases, the clause that follows adds 또 도 ("also/too") to underline the addition.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form on verbs/adjectives: the 으 rule, just like any -(으)ㄹ ending

-(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 attaches to the verb or adjective stem using the same 받침 (final consonant) rule as any other -(으)ㄹ ending: no final consonant → -ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라; final consonant → -을 뿐(만) 아니라. Stems ending in ㄹ (like 살다, 만들다) simply add 뿐(만) 아니라 with no extra ㄹ, since the stem already ends in ㄹ. 이다/아니다 take 일 뿐(만) 아니라 / 아닐 뿐(만) 아니라. The 만 in 뿐만 아니라 is optional — 뿐 아니라 alone is also correct and slightly less emphatic — but 뿐만 아니라 is the more common, more emphatic form you'll see most often in writing.

-(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 by stem type
Stem typeRuleExample
No 받침-ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라크다 → 클 뿐만 아니라
받침 O-을 뿐(만) 아니라많다 → 많을 뿐만 아니라
ㄹ 받침-ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 (no change)살다 → 살 뿐만 아니라
이다/아니다일 뿐(만) 아니라 / 아닐 뿐(만) 아니라학생이다 → 학생일 뿐만 아니라
Study Tip 뿐 by itself is a 의존명사 meaning "only" — it must follow a modifier, never stand alone. You've effectively already met this shape: -(으)ㄹ 뿐이다 ("it is only/merely that ~") uses the exact same -(으)ㄹ + 뿐 structure, just closed with 이다 instead of 만 아니라. Once you see 뿐(만) 아니라 as "-(으)ㄹ 뿐이다, but negated and intensified," the form stops feeling arbitrary.

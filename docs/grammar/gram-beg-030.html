안/-지 않다 Negation

Two Ways to Say "Not" — Short Form vs. Long Form Negation

LevelElementary (TOPIK I, 1–2급) — first item of this stage's Negation & Ability section
CategoryNegation & Ability > 4. Negation & Ability
Learning Goals ① Form short-form negation by placing 안 immediately before a verb/adjective (안 + verb), the more common, casual/spoken form
② Form long-form negation by attaching -지 않다 to a verb/adjective stem (stem + -지 않아요), and recognize the two forms are interchangeable in meaning for most verbs

1Concept Introduction — Two Ways to Say "Not"

This chapter opens a brand-new section, "Negation & Ability (부정·가능)." Korean has two completely regular ways to turn any sentence negative: a short form using the standalone word 안 placed right before the verb, and a long form that attaches -지 않다 onto the end of the verb stem. Both mean exactly the same thing — "not [verb]" — they just differ in where the negative piece goes and how formal/written they feel.

You'll hear the short form 안 constantly in everyday spoken Korean because it's quick and easy: just drop 안 in front of the verb. The long form -지 않다 is a little more deliberate-sounding and shows up often in writing, formal speech, and textbooks, but it is just as correct and just as common to hear. Beginning learners should be comfortable producing and recognizing both.

Preview
저는 커피를 안 마셔요. ("I don't drink coffee.")
저는 커피를 마시지 않아요. ("I don't drink coffee.")
Same meaning, two structures: 안 goes before the verb (안 마셔요), while -지 않다 attaches after the verb stem (마시지 않아요).

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Short-form negation: 안 + verb/adjective

The short form simply places 안 directly in front of the conjugated verb or adjective. Nothing about the verb's own conjugation changes — you just insert 안 right before it.

안 + conjugated verb/adjective
Verb/AdjectiveConjugated안 + conjugatedMeaning
가다 (to go)가요안 가요"don't/won't go"
먹다 (to eat)먹어요안 먹어요"don't eat"
크다 (to be big)커요안 커요"is not big"
좋다 (to be good)좋아요안 좋아요"is not good"

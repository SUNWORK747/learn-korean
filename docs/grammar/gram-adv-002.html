1Concept Introduction — Citing a Cause, the Formal Written Way

This is the second item of Advanced (고급, TOPIK II 5–6급) Korean, right after GRAM-ADV-001's written noun-form ending -(으)ㅁ. -(으)로 인해(서) is one of the most common phrases you'll run into in Korean news headlines, articles, official announcements, and reports — it means "due to," "owing to," or "as a result of," and it attaches only to nouns. It comes from the formal, largely literary verb 인하다 ("to be caused by / to result from," built on the Sino-Korean root 因, "cause"), combined with the connective -아/어(서) you already know from GRAM-BEG-025. 인하 + 아(서) contracts to 인해(서), and the whole phrase is preceded by the noun-attaching particle -(으)로 (GRAM-BEG-010), which points at the cause the way "by means of" points at a method.

You've already met two other ways to give a reason: -기 때문에 (GRAM-INT-031), which attaches to a verb/adjective/이다 clause, and its noun-attaching cousin Noun+때문에, which leans casual and often carries a mild blame or complaint tone ("because of you," "because of the rain" said with a sigh). -(으)로 인해(서) fills a different slot: it attaches only to nouns, like 때문에, but stays neutral and formal, like -기 때문에 — which is exactly why it's the phrase Korean journalists and report-writers reach for instead of either one.

Preview
폭우로 인해 도로가 통제되었습니다. ("Roads were closed due to heavy rain.")
경기 침체로 인해서 실업률이 증가하고 있다. ("Due to the economic downturn, the unemployment rate is rising.")
이 사고로 인한 피해는 막대했다. ("The damage caused by this accident was immense.")
폭우로 인해 (noun 폭우 + -로 인해) names the cause directly in classic news-report style; 침체로 인해서 shows the fuller -인해서 form; 사고로 인한 shows the adnominal form -(으)로 인한, used right before another noun (피해, "damage"). Same root phrase, three faces you'll meet in this chapter.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form: Noun + -(으)로 인해(서)

-(으)로 인해(서) attaches straight to a noun using the same batchim rule as the particle -(으)로: a vowel-ending noun (no final consonant) takes 로 인해(서); a noun ending in a consonant takes 으로 인해(서); and, as with -(으)로 itself, a noun ending in the consonant ㄹ is treated like a vowel-ending noun and also takes plain 로 인해(서). 인해 and 인해서 are largely interchangeable — 인해서 can feel very slightly fuller, but both are completely standard in formal writing.

-(으)로 인해(서) by noun ending
Noun endingRuleExample
Vowel-ending (no batchim)로 인해(서)화재 → 화재로 인해(서) ("due to the fire")
Consonant-ending (batchim, not ㄹ)으로 인해(서)태풍 → 태풍으로 인해(서) ("due to the typhoon")
ㄹ-ending (batchim ㄹ)로 인해(서) — exception, acts like vowel-ending폭설 → 폭설로 인해(서) ("due to the heavy snow")
Key Rule -(으)로 인해(서) attaches only to nouns — never directly to a verb or adjective stem. If the cause is really an action or a state rather than a thing, you either need a noun that names the event (예: 비가 아니라 "비" 자체, 사고, 화재, 지연 등) or you should switch to -기 때문에 (GRAM-INT-031) instead. A common learner error is trying *비가 오로 인해 (✗) — the fix is to use the noun 비 itself: 비로 인해 ("due to the rain"), or nominalize the clause with -음/-ㅁ으로 인해 if the whole event needs to stay a clause.

1Concept Introduction — Thinking Out Loud About What You Might Do

This is the last grammar point of Intermediate (중급) Korean, and it fittingly rounds out a small family of "future plan" patterns you've now met three times. GRAM-INT-015 taught -(으)려고 하다 ("I'm planning to…"), a thought-out intention. GRAM-INT-016 taught -기로 하다 ("I've decided to…"), a firm, already-settled decision or promise. -(으)ㄹ까 하다 is the loosest of the three: it describes something the speaker is only loosely considering — an idea floating around in their head, not yet decided, and very likely to change. It's the Korean way of saying "I'm thinking I might…" or "I was sort of thinking of maybe…."

The pattern is built from -(으)ㄹ까 (the same "-ㄹ까" you may recognize from questions like -(으)ㄹ까요? "shall we…?") plus 하다 ("to do/think"). Literally, 갈까 해요 is close to "I'm thinking, 'should I go?'" — the speaker is turning a question over in their own mind rather than announcing a plan. That self-questioning flavor is exactly what makes -(으)ㄹ까 하다 feel so much softer and more tentative than -(으)려고 하다 or -기로 하다.

Preview
저는 이번 주말에 영화를 볼까 해요. ("I'm thinking I might watch a movie this weekend.")
아직 확실하지 않지만 여행을 갈까 해요. ("It's not certain yet, but I'm thinking about going on a trip.")
볼까 해요 and 갈까 해요 both describe a loose, floating idea — not a plan the speaker has committed to. Compare this to 가려고 해요 ("I'm planning to go," GRAM-INT-015) or 가기로 했어요 ("I've decided to go," GRAM-INT-016): both of those sound far more settled than 갈까 해요.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form: attaching -(으)ㄹ까 하다 to the verb stem

-(으)ㄹ까 하다 attaches to action verbs (동사) — like -(으)려고 하다, it isn't normally used with adjectives or 이다, since a "vague plan" only makes sense for something you might do. The choice between -ㄹ까 하다 and -을까 하다 follows the same familiar vowel/ㄹ/consonant split you've already used for -(으)ㄹ 거예요, -(으)려고 하다, and -(으)ㄹ 것 같다.

-(으)ㄹ까 하다 by stem ending
Stem endingFormExample
Vowel (no 받침)-ㄹ까 하다가다 → 갈까 해요
ㄹ 받침-ㄹ까 하다 (ㄹ stays, no change)만들다 → 만들까 해요
Other consonant 받침-을까 하다먹다 → 먹을까 해요
Study Tip This is the same vowel/ㄹ/consonant split you've used for -(으)ㄹ 거예요, -(으)려고 하다, and -(으)ㄹ 것 같다 — ㄹ 받침 stems already end in ㄹ, so they don't take a second one: 만들다 becomes 만들까 해요, never 만들ㄹ까 해요.

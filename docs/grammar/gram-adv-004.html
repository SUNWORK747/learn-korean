-(으)ㅁ으로써 Formal Means & Method

The Written, Clause-Level Way to Say "By ~ing"

③ Compare -(으)ㅁ으로써 (a clause-level, formal means marker) with the basic noun-level means marker -(으)로 (e.g. 버스로), and know when each is the natural choice

1Concept Introduction — "By Doing," in Writing

-(으)ㅁ으로써 is one of the most common ways to express "means" or "method" in formal Korean writing — essays, news articles, official documents, research papers. It corresponds closely to the English pattern "by ~ing," as in "by exercising consistently, one can maintain good health." You've already met the building block: GRAM-ADV-001 introduced -(으)ㅁ, the nominalizer that turns a verb or adjective into a noun-like form (literally, "the act/fact of doing"). -(으)ㅁ으로써 is simply that same -(으)ㅁ followed by 으로써 ("by means of, through") — so 운동하다 ("to exercise") → 운동함 ("the act of exercising") → 운동함으로써 ("by [the act of] exercising").

Because it's built from a nominalizer, -(으)ㅁ으로써 lets an entire action or event — not just a single noun — serve as the means by which something is accomplished. This is what separates it from the beginner-level means marker -(으)로 (GRAM-BEG-010), which you already know attaches to simple nouns like 버스로 ("by bus"). This chapter also briefly touches on a rarer, closely related form without 써 — plain -(으)ㅁ으로 — which can carry a cause/reason nuance instead of a pure means nuance.

Preview
꾸준히 운동함으로써 건강을 유지할 수 있다. ("By exercising consistently, one can maintain one's health.")
정부는 세금을 낮춤으로써 소비를 촉진하려고 했다. ("The government tried to boost consumption by lowering taxes.")
Both sentences name an action — 운동하다 ("to exercise"), 낮추다 ("to lower") — nominalize it with -(으)ㅁ, then mark it as the means with 으로써. This is written, formal register: you'd see these sentences in a health column or an economics article, not in casual chat.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form: the -(으)ㅁ nominalizer you already know, plus 으로써

Since -(으)ㅁ으로써 = stem + -(으)ㅁ + 으로써, it follows exactly the same attachment rule as -(으)ㅁ from GRAM-ADV-001: a vowel-final stem (or ㄹ-final stem) takes -ㅁ으로써, while a consonant-final stem takes -음으로써. Because the nominalized form always ends in the consonant ㅁ, the following 으로써 never shortens to 로써 the way -(으)로 shortens to 로 after a vowel — the full "-(으)ㅁ으로써" spelling is fixed. Irregular verbs nominalize exactly as they did in GRAM-ADV-001: ㄹ-final stems absorb the ㅁ into a single batchim (살다 → 삶으로써, 만들다 → 만듦으로써), and ㅂ-irregular stems shift ㅂ to 우 before adding ㅁ (돕다 → 도움으로써).

-(으)ㅁ으로써 by stem type
Stem typeRuleBase form-(으)ㅁ으로써
Vowel-final-ㅁ으로써운동하다 (to exercise)운동함으로써
Consonant-final-음으로써읽다 (to read)읽음으로써

Study Tip If you're comfortable with the -(으)ㅁ nominalizer from GRAM-ADV-001, you already know every conjugation rule you need here — -(으)ㅁ으로써 adds nothing new to the nominalizing step itself, only the fixed 으로써 attached after it. In actual usage, -(으)ㅁ으로써 appears overwhelmingly with action verbs (동사); adjectives are rare in this pattern, since "means/method" naturally describes something you do, not a state something is.

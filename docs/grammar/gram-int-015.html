-(으)려고 하다 Plans & Intentions

Saying What You're Planning or Intending to Do

GRAM-INT-015 Intermediate (TOPIK II) 30 min

Learning Goals ① Understand that -(으)려고 하다 expresses a plan or intention to do something that hasn't happened yet, and correctly attach it to verb stems (-려고 하다 after vowels/ㄹ, -으려고 하다 after consonants)

③ Distinguish -(으)려고 하다 from the bare connective -(으)려고 ("in order to," no 하다 needed) and from the neighboring patterns -겠- (GRAM-INT-014) and -기로 하다 (GRAM-INT-016)

1Concept Introduction — Saying What You Plan or Intend to Do

-(으)려고 하다 is how Korean speakers talk about a plan or intention that hasn't happened yet — the closest English equivalent is "to plan to," "to intend to," or sometimes just "to be going to." You already met the future/guess marker -겠- (GRAM-INT-014) and the simpler future -(으)ㄹ 거예요 (GRAM-BEG-016); -(으)려고 하다 adds a more deliberate, thought-out shade of meaning: it's not just "this will happen" but "I have decided this is what I want to do," even if it hasn't started yet and could still change.

The pattern is built from two pieces you can pull apart: -(으)려고 (a connective meaning "in order to" / "intending to") plus 하다 ("to do"). Literally, 가려고 해요 is something like "I do [something] intending to go" — which naturally smooths out to "I'm planning to go." Because -(으)려고 hides inside -(으)려고 하다, this chapter also covers what happens when -(으)려고 appears on its own, without 하다, and a very handy related pattern, -(으)려던 참이다, for saying you were just about to do something.

Preview
저는 내년에 한국에 가려고 해요. ("I'm planning to go to Korea next year.")
친구한테 전화하려고 했는데 깜박했어요. ("I was planning to call my friend, but I forgot.")
가려고 해요 states a plan that hasn't happened yet. 전화하려고 했는데 깜박했어요 uses the past tense -(으)려고 했다 — and shows the classic nuance: a past intention that, in the end, didn't get carried out.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form: attaching -(으)려고 하다 to the verb stem

-(으)려고 하다 only attaches to action verbs (동사) — you can't use it with adjectives or 이다, since you can't "intend" to be pretty or intend to be a student in the same way you intend to do something. Just like -(으)ㄹ 거예요ㆍ-(으)면 and other "으-family" endings, the choice between -려고 하다 and -으려고 하다 depends on whether the stem ends in a vowel/ㄹ or another consonant.

-(으)려고 하다 by stem ending
Stem endingFormExample
Vowel (no 받침)-려고 하다가다 → 가려고 해요
ㄹ 받침-려고 하다 (ㄹ stays)만들다 → 만들려고 해요
Other consonant 받침-으려고 하다먹다 → 먹으려고 해요
Study Tip This is the exact same vowel/ㄹ/consonant split you've already used for -(으)세요, -(으)면, and -(으)ㄹ 거예요 — ㄹ 받침 verbs behave like vowel-final verbs and skip the 으, so 만들다 becomes 만들려고 해요, never 만들으려고 해요.

-(으)ㄹ게요 Intention/Promise

A Personal Commitment Made Right Now, to the Listener

GRAM-BEG-019 Elementary (TOPIK I) 20 min
LevelElementary (TOPIK I, 1–2급)
CategorySentence-Final Endings > 2. Sentence-Final Endings
Learning Goals ① Attach -(으)ㄹ게요 correctly by batchim — ㄹ게요 after a vowel-ending stem, 을게요 after a consonant-ending stem
② Use -(으)ㄹ게요 to express the SPEAKER's own intention or promise, made in the moment of speaking, often in response to a request
③ Distinguish -(으)ㄹ게요 (immediate personal promise/commitment to the listener) from -(으)ㄹ 거예요 (GRAM-BEG-016, general future plan or presumption, can describe anyone)

1Concept Introduction — Making a Promise in the Moment

In GRAM-BEG-016 you learned -(으)ㄹ 거예요, which can talk about the future in two ways: a plan the speaker already had, or a guess about anyone or anything — including the weather, a stranger, or a friend. -(으)ㄹ게요 looks similar (both start with -(으)ㄹ), but it does a much narrower, more personal job.

-(으)ㄹ게요 is what you say when YOU, the speaker, decide right now — on the spot, usually in response to something the listener just said or asked for — that you will do something. It's less "I predict/plan" and more "I promise / I've got it." Because it's a commitment made to the listener, it only ever attaches to something the speaker themselves will do.

Preview
A: 문 좀 닫아 주세요. ("Please close the door.")
B: 네, 닫을게요. ("Okay, I'll close it.")
Speaker B didn't have a plan to close the door before A asked — the decision is made right in that moment, as a direct promise back to A. That instant, personal commitment is exactly what -(으)ㄹ게요 signals.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Attaching 을게요/ㄹ게요 — the batchim rule

-(으)ㄹ게요 attaches to a verb stem (the dictionary form minus 다). Exactly like the batchim rule you already used for -(으)ㄹ 거예요 in GRAM-BEG-016: a vowel-ending stem takes ㄹ게요 directly, while a consonant-ending stem inserts 으 first, giving 을게요.

-(으)ㄹ게요 — attachment rule
Stem endingRuleDictionary formResult
Vowel (받침 없음)+ ㄹ게요하다 (to do)할게요
Vowel (받침 없음)+ ㄹ게요가다 (to go)갈게요
Consonant (받침 있음)+ 을게요먹다 (to eat)먹을게요
Consonant (받침 있음)+ 을게요닫다 (to close)닫을게요
Study Tip This is the same consonant-vs-vowel logic you've now seen in 이에요/예요 (GRAM-PRE-017), 은/는 (GRAM-BEG-001), and -(으)ㄹ 거예요 (GRAM-BEG-016). One extra detail: for stems ending in the ㄹ batchim (e.g. 살다, "to live"), the ㄹ is dropped and treated like a vowel ending — 살다 → 살게요 ("I'll live [there]"), not 살을게요.

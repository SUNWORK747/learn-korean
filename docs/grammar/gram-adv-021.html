-(으)려니와 Acknowledging Plus Addition

The Rare, Literary Way to Say "Granted X Is True — and Y as Well"

GRAM-ADV-021 Advanced (TOPIK II) 30 min
LevelAdvanced (고급, TOPIK II 5–6급) — 2nd item of the "담화·논증 표현" (Discourse/Argumentation Expressions) section, right after GRAM-ADV-020 -되 (조건부 허용)
Category담화·논증 표현 (Discourse/Argumentation Expressions) > 5. 담화·논증 표현
Learning Goals ① Understand -(으)려니와 as a formal, literary connective meaning "granted/acknowledging that clause 1 is true, clause 2 adds a further point" — a 첨가·인정 (addition + acknowledgment) pattern often translated "not only ... but also ..."
② Correctly attach -(으)려니와 to verb/adjective stems (with the 으 rule, including ㅂ 불규칙 stems) and to 이다 as -(이)려니와
③ Recognize -(으)려니와 as a rarer, more literary/classical-flavored alternative to -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 (GRAM-ADV-015) — a pattern to recognize confidently in formal writing and speeches, more than one to actively produce

1Concept Introduction — Granting a Fact, Then Adding to It

In GRAM-ADV-015 you learned -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라, the everyday formal way to say "not only X, but also Y." -(으)려니와 does similar work, but with a distinct flavor: it first acknowledges or grants that the first clause is true — almost like saying "it goes without saying that ..." — and then adds a second, often stronger or complementary point on top of it. This combination of 인정 (acknowledgment) and 첨가 (addition) is why the pattern is glossed as "첨가·인정": you concede clause 1 as an accepted fact, and clause 2 builds on it.

-(으)려니와 belongs to a small, elevated corner of 문어체 (written/literary register) — it shows up in formal essays, speeches, editorials, and classical-leaning prose, but almost never in everyday conversation. Because of that, this chapter's goal is a little different from most: you should learn to recognize -(으)려니와 confidently when you meet it in formal writing, understand its "granted X, and also Y" logic, and be able to form it correctly — but you won't reach for it nearly as often as -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 in your own writing. This is the 2nd item of the "담화·논증 표현" (Discourse/Argumentation Expressions) cluster, right after GRAM-ADV-020 -되 (조건부 허용).

Preview
그는 실력도 뛰어나려니와 성품도 훌륭하다. ("Not only is he highly skilled, but his character is also excellent.")
이 지역은 경치도 아름다우려니와 역사도 깊다. ("This region is not only scenically beautiful, but its history also runs deep.")
Both sentences first grant a notable fact (his skill, the region's scenery) and then add a second, equally notable fact (his character, the region's history) — the core "acknowledge, then add" logic of -(으)려니와.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form: the 으 rule, same pattern as -(으)면/-(으)려고

-(으)려니와 attaches to the verb or adjective stem following the same 받침 (final consonant) rule as other -(으) endings: no final consonant → -려니와; final consonant → -으려니와. Stems ending in ㄹ (like 살다) take -려니와 directly, with no extra 으, since ㄹ 받침 behaves like a vowel-final stem for this rule. 이다/아니다 take 이려니와 / 아니려니와. ㅂ 불규칙 stems (아름답다, 춥다, 덥다, etc.) change ㅂ → 우 before the 으, the same way they do before -(으)면 or -(으)니 — so 아름답다 → 아름다우려니와, not *아름답으려니와.

-(으)려니와 by stem type
Stem typeRuleExample
No 받침-려니와크다 → 크려니와
받침 O-으려니와많다 → 많으려니와
ㄹ 받침-려니와 (no 으)살다 → 살려니와
이다/아니다이려니와 / 아니려니와학자이다 → 학자이려니와
ㅂ 불규칙ㅂ → 우 + 려니와아름답다 → 아름다우려니와
Study Tip Because -(으)려니와 is a rare, literary pattern, you'll meet it almost exclusively attached to adjective stems describing a quality worth praising or noting (뛰어나다, 훌륭하다, 아름답다, 깊다, 넓다, etc.) or to 이다. Verb use exists but is much less common — when you do see it, the logic is identical: grant that the action/state in clause 1 holds, then add clause 2.

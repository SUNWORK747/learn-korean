-는 것 Nominalizer ("The Act/Fact of ~ing")

Turning a Verb Into a Noun

GRAM-BEG-038 Elementary (TOPIK I) 20 min
LevelElementary (TOPIK I, 1–2급) — 4th item of this stage's Essential Expressions section
CategoryEssential Expressions > 5. Essential Expressions
Learning Goals ① Attach -는 것 to a present-tense verb stem to turn an action into a noun phrase ("the act/fact of ~ing," or simply "~ing"), letting a verb function as a sentence's subject or object
② Recognize that -는 것 behaves grammatically like any other noun — it can take particles such as 을/를, 이/가, and 은/는
③ Use -는 것 in the two most common everyday patterns: -는 것을 좋아하다/싫어하다 ("like/dislike doing") and -는 것이 좋다/중요하다 ("it's good/important to do")

1Concept Introduction — Turning a Verb Into a Noun

In English, you can turn a verb into a noun just by adding "-ing": "run" becomes "running," and suddenly it can be the subject of a sentence ("Running is fun") or the object of another verb ("I like running"). Korean verbs don't have a built-in "-ing" form like that — instead, Korean uses a small helper word, 것 ("thing"), attached after the verb with the present-tense modifier -는. The whole chunk -는 것 literally means "the ~ing thing" or "the fact/act of ~ing," and once you build it, it behaves exactly like any ordinary noun.

This is an extremely useful pattern because it unlocks sentences you couldn't make before: "I like reading books," "Exercising is important," "Learning Korean is fun." In every one of these, a verb (read, exercise, learn) needs to act like a noun (a "thing" you can like, or that can "be" important/fun) — -는 것 is exactly the tool that makes that possible.

Preview
저는 책 읽는 것을 좋아해요. ("I like reading books.")
운동하는 것이 중요해요. ("Exercising is important.")
In both sentences, a verb phrase (읽다 "to read," 운동하다 "to exercise") has been turned into a noun phrase with -는 것, and then treated exactly like a normal noun with 을 or 이 attached.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Attaching -는 것 to a present-tense verb stem

-는 것 attaches directly to a verb stem, no batchim rule needed — verb stem + -는 것. This is the same -는 you may recognize from "-고 있다" style present-tense description; here it modifies 것 ("thing") the same way an adjective modifies a noun in English ("the reading thing" = "reading," "the exercising thing" = "exercising").

Verb stem + -는 것 — turning a verb into a noun phrase
Verb (dictionary form)Stem+ -는 것Meaning
읽다 (to read)읽읽는 것"reading / the act of reading"
운동하다 (to exercise)운동하운동하는 것"exercising"
배우다 (to learn)배우배우는 것"learning"
만들다 (to make)만들 → 만드만드는 것"making" (ㄹ drops before ㄴ/는)
Study Tip Think of -는 것 as building an English "-ing" noun in two pieces: first turn the verb into its present-tense modifier form with -는, then tack on 것 ("thing") to make it a noun you can plug into any noun-shaped slot in a sentence.

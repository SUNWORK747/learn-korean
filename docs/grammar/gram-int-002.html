1Concept Introduction — Either This, or That

-거나 is the everyday Korean way to connect two or more actions or states as alternatives — exactly like English "or" and "either…or." You already know -고 (GRAM-BEG-023) for listing things that all happen together ("I ate breakfast and went to school"). -거나 does the opposite job: it presents options where, in most real situations, only one of them actually applies — "On weekends I watch a movie or read a book" doesn't mean both happen every weekend, just that either one might.

The good news is that the form is one of the simplest in Korean grammar: -거나 attaches directly to the stem with no vowel-harmony changes and no irregular conjugation, ever. The part that takes practice is choosing between its two usage patterns — repeating -거나 on both alternatives, or attaching it only once — and knowing where -거나 fits next to endings you already know. This chapter covers both.

Preview
주말에 영화를 보거나 책을 읽어요. ("On weekends, I watch a movie or read a book.")
시간이 없거나 피곤하면 그냥 집에 있어요. ("If I don't have time or I'm tired, I just stay home.")
보거나 (watch + -거나) and 없거나 (don't have + -거나) both present two alternatives — the speaker isn't saying both things happen, just that either one might.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form: always just stem + -거나

-거나 attaches directly to the verb or adjective stem, with no vowel-harmony changes (no -아/어 split) and no irregular conjugation — it is always simply stem + -거나, no matter what the stem ends in. 이다/아니다 take (이)거나/아니거나, and 있다/없다 behave exactly like verbs.

-거나 by word type
Word typeFormExample
Verb (동사)stem + -거나가다 → 가거나, 먹다 → 먹거나
Adjective (형용사)stem + -거나크다 → 크거나, 싸다 → 싸거나
있다/없다stem + -거나있거나, 없거나
이다/아니다(이)거나 / 아니거나학생이거나, 학생이 아니거나
Study Tip Because -거나 starts with a consonant and never triggers vowel changes, it is one of the most predictable endings to conjugate: 하다 becomes 하거나 (never 해거나), and even /르 irregular verbs like 모르다 stay regular here — 모르거나 (never 몰라거나). Also note that tense is normally marked only once, on the final predicate of the whole sentence, not on each clause joined by -거나: 어제 영화를 보거나 책을 읽었어요 ("Yesterday I watched a movie or read a book" — only 읽다 carries the past tense).

1Concept Introduction — Too Busy Doing One Thing to Do Another

Picture the classic excuse: "Sorry I'm late — I was looking for my keys." In Korean, that exact shape of excuse has its own dedicated grammar ending: -느라고. It attaches to an action verb to say "because I was busy doing [this]," and the sentence it introduces almost always ends in something negative or unwanted — being late, missing something, failing to finish, getting scolded. You'll hear -느라고 constantly in spoken Korean any time someone explains why something didn't go as planned.

-느라고 is stricter than the general-purpose reason connectives you already know (-아/어서, -(으)니까): it only works when one action ate up the time or attention that was needed for another action, done by the exact same person. That combination — same subject, time-consuming cause, negative result — is what makes -느라고 its own grammar point rather than just another way to say "because."

Preview
숙제하느라고 텔레비전을 못 봤어요. ("Because I was doing homework, I couldn't watch TV.")
어제 게임하느라고 잠을 못 잤어요. ("Because I was playing games yesterday, I couldn't sleep.")
In both sentences the same person does the -느라고 action (doing homework, playing games) and suffers the negative result (not watching TV, not sleeping) — that's the whole shape of -느라고 in a nutshell.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Form: action verbs only, no tense marker before it

-느라고 attaches directly to an action-verb stem (동사), in the same "present-looking" shape every time — it never takes -았/었- (past) or -겠- (future/presumptive) before it, no matter what tense the overall sentence is in. Descriptive verbs/adjectives (형용사) and 이다 cannot take -느라고 at all, because -느라고 specifically describes spending time doing something, not simply being something.

-느라고 by word type
Word typeCan attach -느라고?Example
Action verb (동사)Yes — stem + -느라고공부하다 → 공부하느라고
ㄹ-irregular verbYes — stem drops ㄹ before -느라고놀다 → 노느라고
Adjective (형용사)No바쁘다 → 바쁘느라고
이다No학생이다 → 학생느라고
Study Tip Never attach past tense -았/었- before -느라고 — 공부했느라고 is incorrect. -느라고 already implies that the cause-action was happening or taking up time before the (usually already-past) result, so any past-tense meaning is carried by the second clause instead: 어제 밤늦게까지 공부하느라고 잠을 못 잤어요 ("Because I studied until late last night, I couldn't sleep"). Also remember the ㄹ-drop rule you already know from other -느/-는/-ㄴ endings: 살다→사느라고, 만들다→만드느라고.

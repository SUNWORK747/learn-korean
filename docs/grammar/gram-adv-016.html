-(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 Strong Negative Conjecture

"There's No Way..." — Rejecting a Possibility on the Strength of Your Own Logic

GRAM-ADV-016 Advanced (TOPIK II) 30 min
LevelAdvanced (TOPIK II, 5–6급) — 1st item of the "추측·전문(傳聞) 고급 표현" (Advanced Conjecture/Hearsay Expressions) section, opening a new 4-chapter cluster (GRAM-ADV-016~019) right after GRAM-ADV-015 -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 closed out the Advanced Cause/Condition section
CategoryAdvanced Conjecture/Hearsay Expressions > 4. 추측·전문(傳聞) 고급 표현
Learning Goals ① Understand that -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 expresses a strong, confident negative conjecture grounded in the speaker's own logic or knowledge ("there's no way/reason that X could be true") — not a soft guess but near-certainty — and know that it literally builds on 리 (理, "reason/cause") + 가 없다 ("there is no")
② Correctly form -(으)ㄹ/을 리가 없다 for verbs, adjectives, 이다, and 있다/없다 in the present and past (-았/었을 리가 없다), and recognize the rare positive counterpart -(으)ㄹ 리가 있다, almost always used as a rhetorical question (-(으)ㄹ 리가 있겠어요?)
③ Compare -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다's near-certain, logic-based negative conjecture with the softer, hedged guess of -(으)ㄹ 것 같다 (GRAM-INT-012) and the perception-based/volitional -겠- (GRAM-INT-014), placing all three on a single confidence spectrum

1Concept Introduction — When a Guess Becomes a Conviction

This chapter opens a brand-new four-part cluster, "추측·전문(傳聞) 고급 표현" (Advanced Conjecture/Hearsay Expressions, GRAM-ADV-016~019), which picks up right after GRAM-ADV-015 -(으)ㄹ 뿐(만) 아니라 wrapped up the Advanced Cause/Condition section. You already know several ways to guess in Korean — the soft, all-purpose -(으)ㄹ 것 같다 and the in-the-moment -겠-. -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 is different in kind, not just degree: it isn't really "guessing" at all. It's the grammar Korean speakers reach for when they want to reject a possibility outright, based on what they personally know or how they reason about a situation — closer to "that can't possibly be true" than to "I think that's probably not true."

The key to understanding the pattern is the dependent noun 리, borrowed from the Chinese character 理 meaning "reason," "cause," or "logical principle." -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 attaches a modifier to 리 and then denies its existence with 없다 ("there is no") — so the whole phrase literally means "there is no reason/logical grounds for [X] to be true." When you tell a friend 그 사람이 거짓말을 할 리가 없어요, you're not hedging — you're saying that, given everything you know about that person, a lie from them is simply not a live possibility. This chapter covers the form, the rare positive counterpart, and how -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 fits alongside the conjecture forms you've already learned.

Preview
그 사람이 그런 실수를 할 리가 없어요. ("There's no way that person would make a mistake like that.")
이 시간에 은행이 문을 닫았을 리가 없어요. ("There's no way the bank is closed at this hour.")
Both sentences reject a possibility outright — not "probably not," but "there's no logical basis for that to be true," based on the speaker's own reasoning (that person's known character; the bank's known hours).

2Concept Explanation

2.1 The origin: 리 (理, "reason") + 가 없다 ("there is no")

리 is a dependent noun (never used alone) that comes from the Sino-Korean root 理, meaning "reason," "cause," or "logical principle" — the same 理 that appears in words like 이치 (理致, "logic, the way things work") and 원리 (原理, "principle"). Grammatically, -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 works exactly like a possessive/existential sentence: a modifier (-(으)ㄹ) attaches to 리 the way it would attach to any noun, 가 marks 리 as the subject, and 없다 ("to not exist") denies it. Read piece by piece, 갈 리가 없어요 is "[the fact of] going → reason for it → [that reason] does not exist" — i.e., "there's no way [he/she/it] would go."

Study Tip One expression built on this pattern is so common it functions almost like a fixed phrase on its own: 그럴 리가 없어요! ("There's no way that could be!"), from 그러하다 ("to be so") + -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다. It's the go-to reaction when someone tells you something you find hard to believe.

3Examples

3.1 -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 across contexts

KoreanMeaningNote
그 사람이 거짓말을 할 리가 없어요."There's no way that person would lie."Present, based on knowing the person's character
그녀가 이 비싼 가방을 샀을 리가 없어요."There's no way she bought this expensive bag."Past, based on knowing her usual spending habits
지금 사무실에 아무도 없을 리가 없어요."There's no way nobody's in the office right now."Present, 있다/없다 base
그가 매일 열심히 공부했으니까 시험에 떨어질 리가 없어요."He studied hard every day, so there's no way he'll fail the exam."Reasoning explicitly stated with -(으)니까 (cause)
그게 사실일 리가 있겠어요?"How could that possibly be true?" (i.e., "there's no way that's true")Rhetorical question, rare positive form

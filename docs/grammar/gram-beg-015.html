-았/었- (과거) Past Tense

Marking Past Tense — Inserted Before the Sentence Ending

GRAM-BEG-015 Elementary (TOPIK I) 20 min
LevelElementary (TOPIK I, 1–2급) — third item in Sentence-Final Endings, building directly on GRAM-BEG-013 and GRAM-BEG-014
CategorySentence-Final Endings > 2. Sentence-Final Endings
Learning Goals ① State that -았/었- is a past-tense marker inserted BEFORE the -아/어요 ending (GRAM-BEG-013), following the same vowel-harmony rule (았 after a bright stem, 었 after a dark stem)
② Conjugate common regular verbs into the past tense, including the irregular 하다 → 했어요 pattern
③ Form the formal past tense by attaching -았/었습니다 instead of -았/었어요, connecting to GRAM-BEG-014's 합쇼체

1Concept Introduction — Same Sentence, One Tense Away

In GRAM-BEG-013 you learned how to end a sentence politely with -아/어요, and in GRAM-BEG-014 you learned the more formal -ㅂ니다/습니다 ending. Both of those, on their own, describe the present or a general fact. To talk about something that already happened, Korean doesn't hand you a whole new set of endings to memorize — it simply slips a small piece, -았/었-, in right before the ending you already know.

This is why -았/었- is called a marker rather than an ending: it sits between the verb stem and the sentence-final ending, like a tense "insert." And the choice between 았 and 었 isn't random — it follows the exact same vowel-harmony rule you already used to build -아/어요 in GRAM-BEG-013.

Preview
가다 ("go"): 가 + 았 + 어요 → 갔어요 ("went")
먹다 ("eat"): 먹 + 었 + 어요 → 먹었어요 ("ate")
Same stems, same ending family you already know — just with -았/었- inserted in between.

2Concept Explanation

2.1 Where -았/었- goes — the same vowel-harmony rule as -아/어요

-았/었- is inserted directly before -아/어요, and it follows the identical vowel-harmony rule taught in GRAM-BEG-013: if the verb stem's last vowel is a bright vowel (양성모음 ㅏ or ㅗ), attach 았; for any other (dark, 음성모음) stem vowel, attach 었.

Choosing 았 vs. 었 — the vowel-harmony rule, reused from GRAM-BEG-013
Stem's last vowelPast markerExample stemResult
ㅏ (bright)았가- (가다, "go")가+았+어요 → 갔어요
ㅗ (bright)았좋- (좋다, "be good")좋+았+어요 → 좋았어요
ㅓ (dark)었먹- (먹다, "eat")먹+었+어요 → 먹었어요
any other dark vowel었ㅜ, ㅡ, ㅣ, etc.stem+었+어요
Study Tip This is the exact same bright-vowel-vs-dark-vowel choice from GRAM-BEG-013: bright stem (ㅏ/ㅗ) → 았; everything else → 었. If you can already conjugate a verb into -아/어요, you already know whether that verb takes 았 or 었 in the past — the rule doesn't change, only one extra piece gets inserted.
